Energy-Efficient Deep Learning for Edge-Based Image Classification

A systematic empirical research investigation evaluating Structured Channel Pruning, Post-Training Dynamic INT8 Quantization, and Teacher-Student Knowledge Distillation on agricultural edge CPU hardware to identify the optimal Pareto frontier model under strict computational and energy budgets.

The Edge Bottleneck

Large vision models (ResNet-50: 90.16 MB, 94.9 ms) drain batteries and exceed the RAM limits of low-cost agricultural drones and microcontrollers.

Scientific Objective

Achieve sub-15ms edge inference latency and sub-10MB storage while maintaining >99% diagnostic accuracy across 23 plant disease categories.

Controlled Testbed

x86_64 Edge CPU (4 threads, batch=1, locked seed=42, 500 benchmark passes). Energy recorded via CodeCarbon; FLOPs via THOP.
